###

<h3 align="left">🛡️ What I Care About</h3>

<p align="left">
I believe AI systems deployed in high-stakes domains — government, security, forensics — need to be interpretable and resilient by design, not as an afterthought. My projects and research lean toward <b>Explainable AI (XAI)</b>, <b>adversarial robustness testing</b>, <b>AI fairness/bias mitigation</b>, and applying <b>digital forensics rigor</b> to ML model behavior. I'm interested in how AI safety, security, and ethics intersect — and in building tools that make that intersection practical rather than theoretical.
</p>
